# Lens: Legal/Compliance

**Focus:** Bar admission in the required jurisdiction. Practice area match.

**Evaluate:** Bar status, practice area depth, billable hours expectations, firm tier, in-house vs. firm experience.

**Watch for:** Jurisdiction requirements (must be barred in a specific state). "Portable" practice areas vs. jurisdiction-specific ones.

**Resume tip:** Bar admissions, practice areas, deal/case values, industry specializations.

**Scoring emphasis:** Credentials are **PASS/FAIL** — bar admission in the required jurisdiction is non-negotiable.

Exact weights and the PASS/FAIL rules live in `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/references/scoring-rubric.md`, which is authoritative.
