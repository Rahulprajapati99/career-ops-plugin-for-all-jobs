# Lens: Sales/Business Development

**Focus:** Numbers. Quota attainment is the primary signal.

**Evaluate:** Quota attainment %, deal size, sales cycle length, territory/vertical match, tech stack (Salesforce, HubSpot).

**Watch for:** Vanity metrics — "managed $10M pipeline" means nothing without a close rate. Hunter vs. farmer role mismatch.

**Resume tip:** Quota attainment percentages. Deal sizes. New logos won. Revenue generated. President's Club recognition.

**Scoring emphasis:** Hard requirements are read as track record — quota attainment replaces a skills list as the primary evidence.

Exact weights and the PASS/FAIL rules live in `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/references/scoring-rubric.md`, which is authoritative.
