# Lens: Trades/Skilled Labor

**Focus:** Certifications, licenses, hands-on experience, safety record.

**Evaluate:** Required licenses/certifications (journeyman, master, CDL class), union status, equipment proficiency, safety record.

**Watch for:** State and local licensing requirements. Union vs. non-union. Specialty certifications (OSHA 30, EPA 608, welding certs).

**Resume tip:** All certifications with numbers and dates. Equipment list. Safety record. Project scale (square footage, tonnage, voltage).

**Scoring emphasis:** Credentials are **PASS/FAIL** — the required journeyman/master license or CDL class is non-negotiable.

Exact weights and the PASS/FAIL rules live in `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/references/scoring-rubric.md`, which is authoritative.
