# Lens: Government/Nonprofit

**Focus:** Clearance level + domain expertise + mission alignment.

**Evaluate:** Security clearance status, GS-level alignment, grant management experience, regulatory knowledge, mission fit.

**Watch for:** Clearance requirements are hard stops — obtaining one can take 6-18 months. Government experience is strongly preferred for federal roles. Nonprofits value mission alignment over credentials.

**Resume tip:** Clearance level and status. Grant amounts managed. Policy areas. Stakeholder types (elected officials, agency heads).

**Scoring emphasis:** Domain and mission fit dominate; compensation alignment is weighted least.

Exact weights and the PASS/FAIL rules live in `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/references/scoring-rubric.md`, which is authoritative.
